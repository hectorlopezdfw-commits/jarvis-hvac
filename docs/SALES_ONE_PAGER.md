# Jarvis HVAC Sales One-Pager

## Product

Jarvis HVAC is an AI-powered lead conversion system for residential and commercial HVAC businesses.

## Problem

Most HVAC businesses lose revenue because they miss calls, respond too slowly, fail to follow up, and do not request reviews after service is complete.

### Common issues

- missed calls become lost jobs
- website leads sit unresponded
- emergency service requests get missed during peak demand
- follow-up is inconsistent
- customer reviews are not collected consistently

## Solution

Jarvis HVAC helps the business respond immediately and keep momentum on every lead.

### It does this by:

- answering leads instantly
- qualifying the service request
- identifying residential vs commercial work
- prioritizing emergency calls
- booking appointments automatically
- sending follow-up reminders to unbooked leads
- requesting Google reviews after service completion

## Business outcome

The system helps contractors increase booked jobs, reduce missed opportunities, and create a more consistent follow-up process without manually chasing every inquiry.

## Who it is for

- residential HVAC companies
- commercial HVAC contractors
- service businesses with inconsistent lead follow-up
- companies losing work due to slow response
