# Discovery Call Script

## Goal

Understand whether the HVAC business is losing leads due to missed calls, delayed follow-up, or inconsistent booking.

## Questions

1. How do most of your new leads come in today?
2. How quickly do you respond to those inquiries?
3. Are missed calls or delayed responses costing you jobs?
4. How do you currently handle follow-up on leads that do not book immediately?
5. Do you have a process for reviewing customers after the job?
6. What does your booking process look like today?
7. What would a system that answered leads instantly and booked more service calls be worth to you?

## Close

Based on what you’re describing, this system sounds like it could help recover missed calls, improve response speed, and increase your booked jobs without adding more admin work to your team.

Would you be open to seeing a quick demo for your business?
